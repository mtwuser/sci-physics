## 1. Progressive `M1 -> M4` Physics Mastery & Application Ladder

| Mastery Stage | Cognitive & Engineering Focus | Core Physical Pillars | Verification Gate (`verify-mastery`) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`M1` (Foundations)** | Physical intuition, free-body diagrams, SI unit consistency (`PHYS-01`), and conservation of energy/momentum. | Newtonian mechanics, work-energy theorem, ideal gas law ($PV = nRT$), basic circuits. | Perform Buckingham $\Pi$ dimensional check on every expression before plugging in numbers. |
| **`M2` (University Rigor)** | Variational principles (`Lagrangian` / `Hamiltonian`), vector fields, and thermodynamic cycles (`sci-maths` `MATH-02` Stokes/Divergence). | Analytical mechanics, Maxwell's equations, Laws of Thermodynamics (`PHYS-02`), wave & Schrödinger equations. | Derive equations of motion from $\mathcal{L} = T - V$ and verify asymptotic limits ($v/c \to 0$, $\hbar \to 0$). |
| **`M3` (Applied Engineering)** | Coupled transport phenomena (heat, mass, momentum), sensor telemetry, and thermal safety cut-points (`PHYS-03`). | Fourier/Fick transport, Reynolds/Nusselt/Sherwood correlations, SHM vibration analysis (`dept-iso-eng`, `dept-iso-bio`). | Validate boundary conditions, thermal safety locks (`< 40.0 °C`), and sensor tolerances. |
| **`M4` (Frontier Research)** | Non-equilibrium statistical physics, symplectic integrators, and quantum/relativistic field theories. | Liouville/Boltzmann transport, Noether gauge symmetries, multiphase pyrolysis kinetics. | Publish models with full `citation_receipt` provenance (`sci-physics::docs/...:E3`). |
